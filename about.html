About the Project

The Nepali Cuisine Interpreter is a smart digital platform designed to make the diverse cuisine of Nepal accessible to everyone. Whether you want to learn about traditional dishes, understand their cultural significance, or master their pronunciation, our tool offers an interactive and educational experience.

Key Features

  • Interactive Search: Enter any Nepali dish (Romanized or Devanagari) for instant results.
  • Multi-Model Accuracy: Uses Rule-Based, TF-IDF, and Ensemble Models to give reliable results, even with fuzzy spelling.
  • Cultural Insights: Learn interesting facts and dietary information about each dish.
  • Pronunciation Guide: Phonetic breakdowns for authentic pronunciation.
  • Visual Representation: Dish images for better understanding and exploration.

Why We Built This

Nepal's cuisine is incredibly diverse, but many dishes are unknown outside the country and even within regions. This tool was built to help learners, tourists, and food enthusiasts explore authentic Nepali dishes confidently, combining modern AI technology with traditional culinary knowledge.

Our Approach

  • Rule-Based Model: Exact matches using predefined rules.
  • TF-IDF Model: Fuzzy matching with semantic understanding of dish names.
  • Ensemble Model: Combines strengths of both models for the most accurate results.

Start Exploring!

Dive in, search for your favorite Nepali dishes, and discover the stories, culture, and flavors behind every bite! Tip: If a dish isn’t recognized, double-check the spelling or try searching only authentic Nepali dishes.
